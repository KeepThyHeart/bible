# KeepThyHeart Bible Desktop v1.0.0-beta.2

This is a beta release, so expect rough edges!  Problems and suggestions are welcome in the Issues tab, or through **Help > Give feedback** in the app.

## Upgrading from 1.0.0-beta.1

Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, settings and modules are kept.

- **Windows:** use the in-app update, or run the new installer.
- **Linux:** install the new `.deb` over the old one, or replace the AppImage.
- **macOS:** download the new `.dmg` and replace the app in Applications.

## What's new and changed since Beta.1

New features, and features that work differently from 1.0.0-beta.1:

### Reading

- The Bible toolbar shows the current book and chapter. Click it to jump anywhere, such as from deep in a long chapter. Tick **Also choose a verse** under the chapter grid to pick a verse as well (it stays on until you untick it), and **Ctrl+G** opens the picker.
- In Study and Parallel views, the chapter title opens the book and chapter picker, and arrows step to the previous or next chapter.
- **Ctrl + mouse wheel** over the Bible text changes the text size.
- The gear in the Bible toolbar opens a panel of Bible text settings: text size, font, line height, red letters, text width, verse labels and scrollbar size. Changes show in the text straight away, and **All text settings…** opens the full Preferences.
- New **Text width** setting (narrow, medium, wide or full pane), set separately for Standard, Reading and Study modes, so Standard can fill the pane while Reading keeps a narrower column. It is in the gear panel and under Preferences > Typography > Reading layout.
- New **Verse labels** setting: verse number, chapter:verse, or book chapter:verse. It is in the gear panel and under Preferences > Typography > Reading layout.
- A curated list of readable fonts that are already installed on your computer is offered under Fonts.
- Scrollbars are larger and visible in every theme, with a Standard/Large choice under Preferences > General.

### Highlights and bookmarks

- Four new highlight colours: pink, teal, gray and brown.
- The floating toolbar's **Underline** button underlines in the colour you last underlined with (red at first), and its icon shows that colour. The small square beside it opens a row of colours to underline in another.
- The **Recent** row keeps your last four highlight and underline styles.
- Highlights are a little stronger in most themes, staying within readable contrast.
- Highlights and underlines are easier to remove: **Remove** in the highlight menu, **Clear highlights and underlines** in the verse menu, and **Ctrl+Shift+X**. Ctrl+Shift+X, and clearing several verses from the menu, ask for confirmation first.
- Bookmarking a dragged selection saves the whole range, and opening a range bookmark selects the whole range. With several verses selected, the bookmark menu says **Add Selected Passage**.

### Study, copy and print

- The Dictionary pane's **Overview** leads with a word search across every installed dictionary. Results are grouped by dictionary, and a word's Strong's entry comes first when it is tagged. The list of dictionaries below it has its own small filter.
- **Look up a word** from the context menu or the floating toolbar opens the Overview with that word's results.
- **Print** selected verses from the Copy Passage dialog or the verse context menu, or press **Ctrl+P** in the Bible pane. The Print dialog shows a live preview of the page beside its settings: layout (numbered verses or block quote), verse numbers, title, colour theme (with optional words of Christ in red), line spacing, text size, and an optional margin for notes in centimetres or inches. Your choices are remembered. **More options…** prints in any of the copy formats instead.
- **Save as PDF…** in the Print dialog suggests a file name from the passage, such as `John 3-16 KJV.pdf`.
- New copy style, **Full reference on first verse**: (John 3:16) on the first verse and bare numbers on the rest, or (John 3:16, KJV) with the version shown.

### Notes

- Adding a note to a verse no longer creates an empty file. The note is saved once you type something. Only notes with content show a marker in the Bible text, and empty book and chapter folders are hidden under Verse Notes.
- Delete a verse note with the trash button in its header, or from the right-click menu in the notes list. Deleting always asks first.
- **Compile passage notes** gathers your notes for a list of passages (such as `Romans 8:1-11; 1 Cor 15:50-58`) into one document, with optional verse text, headings and verse labels. Create it as a new note, print it, or export it to PDF or Word. Open it from the command palette, or by right-clicking a folder under Verse Notes.
- **Ctrl + mouse wheel** over a note, or the zoom control in the notes toolbar, makes the note larger or smaller on screen (50-300%) without changing its font sizes.
- Text direction, subscript and superscript have moved to the notes toolbar's **More** button.

### Search

- Search no longer switches to meaning search on its own. When a keyword search finds few or no results, **Try Ideas Search** is offered.
- The search type is a menu next to the search box.
- The online search consent prompt adds **Not now** and **Don't use the online service**.
- When an Ideas search phrase appears word for word in the Bible, a link offers **Switch to Keyword Search**.
- Search tabs have a pin icon. Click it to pin the tab, so the next search opens in a new tab instead of replacing it.

### Privacy

- Search tabs and reading history are now kept only for the current session by default. To keep them between sessions, turn on **Reopen search tabs on startup** and **Remember reading history between sessions**. There is also a new **Clear history** action in Preferences.

### Modules

- In the Module Manager, the Bundles tab is now **Collections**, and feature packs (`.biblepack`) install through **Upload Module**.
- First run offers **Install from files instead** for computers without internet access.
- About screens and module details now credit SWORD/CrossWire and show each module's source, copyright and licence.

### Help

- **Give feedback** is in the Help panel and the Help menu, for sending a suggestion or an idea. Feedback is sent right away, or queued and sent later if the connection fails.

## Fixes

- Newly installed Bibles appear without restarting the app.
- Building a keyword index no longer freezes the window.
- Right-click menus in the notes pane open at the pointer, not further down and to the right.
- A pane's scrollbar can be grabbed right up to the pane border; the border's resize handle no longer covers part of it.
- The web app no longer sometimes hangs on the splash screen after an update.
