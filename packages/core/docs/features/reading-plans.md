# Reading plans

`src/ReadingPlans/` is the reading-plan engine (task 0073): plans as data, a stock library, a custom plan builder,
the scheduler (today's reading, falling behind, catching up) and the store and service both apps wrap. Pure
TypeScript, exported as the `ReadingPlans` namespace from both `@bible/core` and `@bible/core/browser`.

## Plans are verse ranges

A reading is an inclusive verse range in one book, so a day can be a few verses, a split chapter (Psalm 119), part of a
one-chapter book (Jude over three days), selected verses (Proverbs 3:5-6) or a chronological cut through several books.
A `PlanDefinition` always holds its materialised days; a user plan also keeps its `spec`.

## Builder

The scope (ordered ranges, or parallel `tracks`) is ordered `as-listed`, `canonical` (sorted, merged) or `chronological`
(intersected with the shipped chronological order). The pace is a number of days, an end date (counting only reading
days), chapters a day or verses a day. `split: 'chapter'` cuts only at chapter ends (or the end of a listed piece),
choosing the cut nearest the verse-balanced target; when there are more days than chapters it falls back to verse
splitting. `split: 'verse'` cuts anywhere within 30% of the target, preferring the end of a listed piece, then a
chapter end. Tracks are built to the same number of days and zipped. Deterministic: the same spec always gives the same days.

## Stock library

| Key | Source |
|---|---|
| `stock:canonical-1y` | Genesis-Revelation, 365 days, whole chapters |
| `stock:ot-nt-1y` | OT and NT tracks, 365 days |
| `stock:chronological-1y` | Whole Bible in chronological order, 365 days, verse split |
| `stock:mcheyne` | M'Cheyne's 1842 calendar (public domain), four tracks |
| `stock:nt-90`, `stock:gospels-30`, `stock:psalms-proverbs-31` | Short plans |

The chronological order (`stock/chronological.json`, 790 ranges covering every verse once) follows Ussher's *Annals of
the World* (1650), reading the KJV as literally accurate, with parallel accounts side by side; its `basis` field lists the
placement choices. It and `mcheyne.json` are generated by data builders kept outside this repo (bible-scripts).
`ReadingPlans.test.ts` pins a fingerprint of every stock plan: a change that moves a stock plan's days must bump its
`version`. Enrollments snapshot a stock plan when started, so a bump never moves a reader already in it.

## Pacing

- **Flexible** (default): today is the first unread day; nothing is ever overdue; the estimated finish moves.
- **Fixed**: plan day N falls on the N-th reading day from the start date. Unread earlier days are "behind" (with
  `missedDays` and a catch-up suggestion). The reader can reschedule from today (`shiftSchedule`, read days stay read)
  or switch to flexible (the UI confirms that missed days are then no longer tracked).
- Reading days (weekdays) are per enrollment; rest days get no reading on a fixed schedule.
- After a fixed plan's scheduled end, every unread day counts as missed and the first one is shown.
- `pause`/`resume`: a paused plan leaves Today; resuming a fixed plan restarts its schedule from today, so the
  paused days are not counted as missed. There is no "skip day" action: rescheduling covers it.

## Persistence

Owner `app:reading-plans` in `user_data_item`: `plans` (user plans), `snapshots` (`stock:<id>@<version>`),
`enrollments`, and `completions` (one row per ticked reading, key `<enrollment>/<day>/<reading>`, so backup merges and
sync are a union). No new table; backups already cover `user_data_item`. The legacy `reading_plan*` tables in
`UserDatabase.sql` are not used.

**Web:** web is read-only for personal content until web accounts (0063) exist, so the web app does not offer
plan progress yet; the engine is browser-safe and ready for it.

## Events and scope

`ReadingPlanService.subscribe` emits `changed`, `readingCompleted`, `dayCompleted` and `planCompleted`.
`IReadingScopeProvider.getTodayScope()` gives the current day's readings of every active plan (for the quiz, 0074).
