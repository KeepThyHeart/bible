Faith for Ordinary Days

A short devotional on trust, patience and the plain commands of the gospel.

Where trust begins

Most of us learn the gospel in one sentence: "For God so loved the world" (John 3:16). It is worth reading the surrounding verses too, so see John 3:14-18 and then compare Romans 5:8, which says the same thing from the other side. Paul adds in Rom. 8:28 that all things work together, and in 1 John 4:8 that God is love.

Jesus himself said it plainly in John 14:6. His longer teaching is in Matthew 5:3-12 (the Beatitudes) and, for the quiet reader, Psalm 23 is still the best place to start. The shortest letter is Jude 3, and the shortest chapter is Psalm 117. Read 2 Timothy 1:7 aloud when you are afraid; read II Corinthians 5:17 when you are tired.
